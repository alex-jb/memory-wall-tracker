## Thesis breakdown

### AVGO — the silent winner
Broadcom's custom AI ASIC + networking switch business has been the under-priced narrative vs NVDA. Hyperscaler datacenters require massive switching capacity (1.6T+ tomahawk switches) AND the custom ASIC business (Google TPU successor, Meta MTIA). Druckenmiller's overweight here signals he reads this as a structural multi-year tailwind, not a cyclical bet.

### INTC — Pat Gelsinger fade is now priced in
After 5 years of fab cycle pain, the AI server CPU market is showing signs of recovery. New foundry process node milestones in 2026 H2 plus the data center recovery cycle are the bull case. Druckenmiller's smaller position size (~3%) signals "asymmetric upside, manageable downside" rather than conviction long.

### ARM — design wins compound
Datacenter Neoverse design wins (AWS Graviton 5, NVIDIA Grace 2, Microsoft Cobalt, Alibaba Yitian) are accelerating. ARM captures royalty on each chip shipped — the licensing model means revenue compounds without capex burden. P/E looks rich (55×) but the EPS denominator grows fast.

### MU — direct HBM play
The most direct memory-wall thesis. HBM3E pricing has been firm through 2026 Q1 due to constrained supply. Micron's HBM4 ramp in 2026 H2 lines up with NVIDIA Blackwell-Ultra cycle. Druckenmiller's ~5.5% position reflects high conviction this is the cleanest play.

### STX + WDC — under-loved storage tier
The forgotten basket. AI training requires massive data lakes (petabytes per model). Mass storage hard drives + NAND get demand pull from this. Both trade at 8-10× forward P/E — value pricing for a structural growth narrative. Risk: if SSD margins compress, both names suffer.
